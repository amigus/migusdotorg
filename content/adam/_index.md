I've been writing software since I was a kid, starting with BASIC on an [8088](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intel_8088) my parents brought home in 1985. That machine taught me something I've carried ever since: the best way to understand a system is to take it apart and put it back together again. I remember reading [Yahoo!](https://www.yahoo.com/) in [Mosaic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosaic_(web_browser)) when the whole web fit in a bookmark file. I learned [C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C_(programming_language)) on a Sun [SPARCstation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SPARCstation) in university, graduated with a B.Sc. in Computer Science from [Memorial University](https://mun.ca) in 2001, and have spent the decades since building infrastructure, automating the painful parts, and occasionally writing about what I learned along the way.

In 2014 I founded [The Migus Group](https://migusgroup.com), an IT services firm. Most of my day-to-day work lives in the space between systems — LDAP directories talking to mail servers, DNS feeding DHCP, Ansible orchestrating fleets. The posts here are mostly notes to my future self, published in case they're useful to someone else too.

I publish code as [Gists](https://gist.github.com/amigus) and on [GitHub](https://github.com/amigus). I sign my commits and releases with this [GPG key](/adam/public.asc). You can also find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/amigus/) — though I'd rather you just [email me](mailto:adam@migus.org).
