I am a software engineer :computer:, cybersecurity expert :lock:, computer scientist :scientist:, and information technologist :wrench:.
I'm also an old :older_man:.
I got my first computer :desktop_computer: — an [8088](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intel_8088) — in 1985!
I learned to program in [BASIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_BASIC)
and use [MS-DOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MS-DOS) on it :keyboard:.
I started programming in [C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C_(programming_language))
on a Sun [SPARCstation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SPARCstation) in 1993 :sun:.
I did not own that computer, at least, not in the literal sense,
but boy did I have fun with it! :wink: :smile:
I used [Mosaic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosaic_(web_browser))
to read [Yahoo!](https://www.yahoo.com/) in 1995 :globe_with_meridians: but I spent more time using [Archie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archie_(search_engine)), [FTP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File_Transfer_Protocol) and [Telnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telnet) back then :floppy_disk:.

I got a B.Sc. in Computer Science from [Memorial University](https://mun.ca) in 2001 :mortar_board:.
From there I went to work for a federally funded technology lab that no longer exists :shrug:.
But not all was lost, the contributions I made to the FreeBSD [MAC Framework](https://docs.freebsd.org/en/books/arch-handbook/mac/) while working there were free and open source :penguin:.
I have continued to create and contribute to various open source projects over the years :heart:.
I publish what I create in [Gists](https://gist.github.com/amigus)
and on [GitHub](https://github.com/amigus).
I use this [GPG key](/adam/public.asc) to sign my commits and releases :key:.

I have a [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/amigus/) profile :briefcase: where I detail my professional experience which includes 8 years in software security and architecture and engineering roles in financial services :bank: before ultimately founding my IT services company, [The Migus Group](https://migusgroup.com) in 2014 :office:, to enable me to contract independently and provide specialized IT services to a range of clients. I'm also on [X](https://x.com/amigus) where I share updates and insights about whatever I'm working on. Currently that's [CloudServers.app](https://cloudservers.app) :cloud: and a forthcoming open-source AI/LLM agent harness framework "Lisa." :robot:
